# Hall art

Only the three sprite sheets used by the production hall are included.

- `roguelikeChar_transparent.png` comes from Kenney's **Roguelike Characters
  2.0** pack. The hall composes 16×16 body, shirt, hair, and pointed wizard-hat
  tiles. See `LICENSE-roguelike-characters.txt`.
- `roguelikeSheet_transparent.png` comes from Kenney's **Roguelike/RPG pack**.
  The hall uses its floor, wall, table, chair, door, food, banner, barrel,
  shelf, plant, and couch tiles. See `LICENSE-roguelike-rpg-pack.txt`.

- `roguelikeIndoor_transparent.png` comes from Kenney's **Roguelike Indoors** pack.
  The lounge uses its planks, sofas, armchairs, card chairs, tables and candelabra.
  See `LICENSE-roguelike-indoors.txt`.

All three packs are by Kenney and released under CC0 1.0:

- <https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-characters>
- <https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack>
- <https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-indoors>

The character and RPG sheets were copied unchanged from the accepted hall art.
The indoor sheet and its license were copied unchanged from the approved lounge art.
